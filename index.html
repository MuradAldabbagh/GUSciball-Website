About Us

Bringing together Glasgow University's STEM students for an unforgettable evening!
We are the Glasgow University Science Ball, a student-led, multi-societal committee dedicated to hosting an annual celebration for STEM students across the University of Glasgow.

Organised by:

Physics Society - Astronomy Society - G-Gnomes - Alchemists - WISTEM - Earth Sciences - QuantComp

What to Expect

Dust off your kilts and polish your dancing shoes because the Science Ball is back for the fifth year running!

The event is strictly Black Tie

With purchase of a ticket, you get a 3 course meal, half a bottle of wine, photography and a photobooth, raffle prizes, and a ceilidh band.
